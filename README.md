### Analysis

**[The Alzheimer's drug that came out a year after its rival already costs more to the Medicare the government runs](https://ivandujaut.com/en/projects/leqembi-kisunla)** · 4 min
Kisunla treats fewer patients than Leqembi and costs traditional Medicare more. I
matched spending, the prices Medicare recognizes, the labels and what a site charges
per infusion to find out why, and what the company that is losing would do about it.

**[Five companies already decided Xarelto's future in Medicare, and nobody has seen it](https://ivandujaut.com/en/projects/acceso-parte-d)** · 10 min
USD 2.29 billion a year. The 2027 formularies are already filed and not yet
published. I matched them against enrollment, negotiated prices and the status of
the generics to see what a market access team would do before the year closes.

**[The largest health insurer in the United States denied one in three medical bills](https://ivandujaut.com/en/projects/denegaciones-unitedhealth)** · 9 min
The figure that defined UnitedHealthcare, rebuilt from the files CMS publishes:
224,202 members out of 51.7 million. It fell fourteen points the following year,
across the thirteen entities present in both.
