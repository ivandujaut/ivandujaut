### Analysis

**[PIX did not kill the wallets. It forced them to become banks.](https://ivandujaut.com/en/projects/pix-brasil)** · 8 min
Brazil's free payment rail killed instruments, not companies: the DOC transfer went from 250M
operations a year to exactly zero. Every figure recomputes from the central bank's open data with
[the script in this repo](https://github.com/ivandujaut/ivandujaut/blob/main/scripts/analysis/pix-brasil.py).

**[The premium that never arrives](https://ivandujaut.com/en/projects/cobranza-seguros)** · 10 min
Argentine insurers lose policies to arrears their own process manufactures. Built on the regulator's
monthly filings, with the distinction that changes what you do about it.
